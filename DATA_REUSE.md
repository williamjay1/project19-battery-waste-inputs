# Data reuse: source-specific, not a blanket open-data licence

Official policies were checked on 5 October 2026. This is a conservative release inventory, not legal advice or a new grant of rights over third-party data.

| Source / public files | Policy and transformation | Release decision |
|---|---|---|
| Eurostat Comext DS-045409; `datasets/`, trade-only `results/` CSVs | [Eurostat policy](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice) permits credited reuse subject to exceptions. **Austria-declared CN8 data cannot be reused commercially under the general permission.** Original directions are harmonised to origin → destination; quantity in 100 kg is multiplied by 0.1 to tonnes; missing mirrors remain unknown. | Included for credited non-commercial research reproduction, with exceptions retained. Neither MIT nor CC0 is asserted over these data. |
| UBA; `results/scope_identification_*/uba_pilot_rows.csv` | [UBA section C](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/datenschutz-haftung-urheberrecht) distinguishes data reuse from the CC BY-NC-ND licence over authored text/graphics. The data provision permits transformation and transmission with UBA credit and a change notice unless individually marked otherwise. Numeric PDF table rows are extracted for NL/BE routes and battery codes. | Included as credited transformed numeric data. No PDF artwork, complete report text or logos are redistributed. No blanket relicensing of UBA material. |
| ILT; `results/public_permit_field_gate_20261002_v1/public_permit_scope_cases.csv` | [ILT policy](https://www.ilent.nl/service/copyright) states CC0 for site content except specifically copyrighted material, with separate image restrictions and a non-endorsement condition. The selected public tabular records contain non-personal notification/scope/status/route fields only; no contrary notice was identified for this extract. | Included with source and snapshot credit, subject to those exceptions. Raw ODS is withheld to avoid unnecessary personal/company data redistribution. Planned amounts are not receipts. |
| Basel Germany reports 2023/24 | [Basel terms](https://www.basel.int/Home/TermsofUse/tabid/10656/Default.aspx) allow personal non-commercial inspection/copying but do **not** provide a general redistribution or derivative-work grant. | Raw HTML, reconstructed rows, concordance, interval/certificate/pressure CSVs and related display data/figures are **not** distributed. Scripts, source locators and non-reversible fingerprints are provided. Generate outputs locally only under the applicable terms. |
| Original code, tests, explanatory software documentation | Created for this project. Synthetic test examples are labelled by purpose; they are not analysis data. | MIT, except specifically attributed third-party assets. |
| Nature Figure panel auditor / nature style | Apache-2.0, pinned commit; see third-party notices. | Licence and attribution retained. SciencePlots is an installed dependency, not a re-licensed package. |

Copyright/database rights, source contract conditions and privacy are distinct. A DOI confirms persistent identification, not data accuracy, legal permission or peer review. If broader raw/Basel-derived redistribution is desired, obtain explicit permission first; it is not silently assumed here. No permission request is sent by the release scripts.

Do not treat this repository's licence badge or Zenodo's software-level MIT metadata as an unrestricted licence for embedded external data. Preserve this file and the per-file licence map in any redistribution.
