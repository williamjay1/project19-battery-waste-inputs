# Estimand and evidence boundaries

The primary estimand is the eligible declared input within selected report rows, conditional on explicit chemistry descriptions and declared operations. Bounds are identification sets, not confidence intervals. The reports cannot identify actual EV battery mass, Li-ion-only mass, received shipments, recovered elemental mass or actual facility ranking.

- EU-27 Comext 2022–2025 supplies accounting context, not independent material truth. Missing observation is not zero; export and import views are not added.
- The NL→DE and BE→DE A1170 applications use 2023 and 2024 national-report records. UBA–Basel concordance is same-system corroboration, not independent physical validation.
- The 2023 exact description/stage rule is unchanged in 2024. This is same-system temporal transfer, not preregistration or a physical holdout.
- All 160 deterministic assumption stresses are retained, including non-strict or negative outcomes. They are not Monte Carlo draws and are not assigned probabilities.
- The retained 80 trade diagnostics and the 2025 adverse temporal comparison are not removed or represented as newly designed confirmatory experiments.
- The two original Comext retrievals share an upstream release. Their agreement does not estimate revision stability across releases.
- ILT authorisations, objections and multiyear planned amounts cannot be added as realised annual receipts. Permit case records explain mechanisms, not EU-wide error rates.
- Public numerical data are administrative observations; unit tests use expressly synthetic edge cases. Reproduction validates computations, not the physical truth of administrative declarations.

The public release omits material whose redistribution is not covered by an explicit source permission. The local full replay remains possible from the cited public sources, subject to their current availability and permitted use. If a source disappears or changes, the replay reports it rather than replacing data silently.
